# BarBuddy: cocktail research for the current inventory

Researched 2026-11-16 against all 54 inventory entries in `shared-bar.json`.
This report records the discovery pass. The subsequent import adds 21 measured
recipes/adaptations and 12 discovery links; see BARBUDDY.md for the import record. Recipe names and brief factual
notes are recorded with links; photographs and descriptive prose were not copied.

## How to read the results

“Available base” means the core ingredients and technique are supported by the
owner-confirmed inventory. It does not promise an exact reproduction: brand,
diet-soda, fresh-citrus and garnish differences are called out separately. “Needs”
identifies missing supplies or tools; it is not an exhaustive shopping cart for
pages that are only indexed or incomplete. A shaker and mixing glass remain
unconfirmed. Fresh citrus, sugar, milk and cream have not been assumed.

Each inventory row was considered, including premixed cocktails, garnishes and
ice. Brand-specific recipes are preferred. Category matches and adaptations are
explicit; a product page or recipe collection is only a lead. No verified match
means no suitable exact product recipe was established in this pass, not that
none exists anywhere online. Public pages and creator/community sources were used;
login-only social videos were not treated as verified recipes.

## Most useful first candidates

1. **Toki Highball:** strongest exact-bottle match; core supplies on hand. Fresh citrus garnish is unconfirmed.
2. **Fireball & Ginger / Fireball & Coffee:** official simple recipes; prepare the diet ginger ale or brew/chill coffee first.
3. **Shirley Temple / Dirty Shirley:** SodaStream publishes the soda/grenadine/cherry combination with optional vodka. Use your diet version and separate the alcoholic variant.
4. **Empress & Tonic:** exact gin, prepared tonic; grapefruit garnish would be omitted or changed.
5. **Kalimotxo:** useful for all three confirmed red wines and the cola concentrate; identify it as the diet-cola version.
6. **Portland Pomegranate Cherry Sunrise:** vendor supports grenadine use; stocked tequila and bottled OJ make a practical adaptation.

These assessments are research judgments, not the current app’s automatic matching
results. Existing matching still distinguishes bottled/fresh juice and brand-specific
ingredients. Some vendor pages list ingredient names but omit quantities or method;
those must stay out of the make-now calculation until completed.

## Import plan

Start with the complete, short vendor recipes and retain a source URL, publisher,
creator credit, checked date, measured ingredient requirements, method, tools,
optional garnishes, and explicit substitutions on every record. Deduplicate
against the existing 667-drink catalog before adding anything.

Keep source recipes and BarBuddy adaptations separate. Prepared SodaStream mixers
can use the existing conversion rules; bottled juice, diet soda, different gin
variants, and different amari should remain visible choices. Use `discovery-only`
for incomplete/video-only recipes and unresolved product matches.

A useful inventory interaction is “Drinks using this item,” showing three groups:
ready with current bar; possible with disclosed substitutions; missing ingredients
or equipment. Each result should link directly to its source. Shopping should
operate on the missing ingredient set, not assume the vendor’s entire brand list
must be purchased.

Do not bulk-copy vendor photos or prose under the Bar Assistant MIT license.
That license covers its own dataset, not these external websites. Keep external
photos as outbound source links unless reuse permission or another suitable
license is established. No paid recipe subscription is needed for these links.

## Verification limits and corrections

- Portland’s current product page explicitly says Pomegranate Cherry works as
  grenadine. That is stronger evidence than the earlier conservative inventory
  note; an approved adaptation can use it without equating it to cherry liqueur.
- SodaStream’s Shirley Temple page lists vodka as optional, but its method adds
  vodka unconditionally. BarBuddy should resolve this by storing separate
  nonalcoholic and vodka variants, not reproducing the inconsistency.
- Scrappy’s “After Dinner Drink” appears under Aromatic in its directory but its
  readable ingredient list omits bitters. It was rejected in favor of the vendor’s
  Old Fashioned lead. Never infer ingredients solely from a collection tag.
- No exact Noble ginger blend, Honest Kids punch, or Dragonfly jasmine essence
  cocktail was verified. Tallax still needs identification. Jun Ho lychee-soju
  recipes are different-brand leads for Soonhari, not exact matches.
- Simply and LCBO pages had access/cache failures; indexed leads are labeled and
  should be rechecked before ingestion. Links are not evidence of live shopping stock.
